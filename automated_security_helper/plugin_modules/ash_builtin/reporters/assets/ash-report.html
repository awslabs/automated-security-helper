How to read scanner results

Actionable means a finding meets or exceeds its scanner's configured threshold. Suppressed findings do not contribute to this count.

PASSED has no actionable findings. FAILED exceeds the threshold. MISSING needs dependencies. SKIPPED did not run (disabled, or nothing to scan). ERROR could not complete. Coverage notices above explain partial results.

Threshold sources: global settings, scanner config, or the scanner default.
